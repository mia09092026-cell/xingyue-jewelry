# XINGYUE 星悦 Homepage Design

## Goal

Build a polished bilingual homepage for `XINGYUE 星悦`, a jewelry independent site focused on moissanite, lab-grown diamond jewelry, S925 silver settings, and custom K gold settings.

The page should feel simple, luxurious, and softly romantic, with a moonlight-inspired visual direction suitable for fine jewelry shoppers.

## Audience

The homepage targets international and Chinese-speaking customers who want elegant, affordable fine jewelry with transparent materials, certificate support, and customization options.

## Brand Direction

- Brand name: `XINGYUE 星悦`
- Placeholder domain: `xingyuejewelry.com`
- Tone: refined, calm, luminous, trustworthy
- Visual style: moonlight luxury, using pearl white, soft silver, mist blue, champagne gold, and deep ink accents
- Language: bilingual Chinese and English, with English leading and Chinese supporting

## Page Structure

1. Header navigation
   - Brand mark: `XINGYUE 星悦`
   - Navigation: Home, Collections, Custom, Certificates, Reviews, Contact
   - Primary action: View Collection

2. Hero banner
   - Large first-viewport brand statement
   - Bilingual headline for moissanite, lab-grown diamonds, and S925 silver jewelry
   - Supporting custom K gold setting message
   - Two calls to action: Shop Collection and Custom Design
   - Elegant jewelry-inspired visual composition

3. Product categories
   - Rings / 戒指
   - Necklaces / 项链
   - Earrings / 耳饰
   - Bracelets / 手链
   - Custom K Gold / K 金定制

4. Materials and customization
   - Moissanite brilliance
   - Lab-grown diamond sustainability
   - S925 silver setting value
   - K gold custom setting option

5. Lab-grown and moissanite advantages
   - Ethical and traceable
   - Excellent sparkle and durability
   - Better value for daily luxury
   - Customizable stone size, cut, and setting

6. Certificate and assurance section
   - Placeholder references for IGI, GIA, NGTC, and S925 material marks
   - Clear explanation that certificate availability depends on stone type and order

7. Customer reviews
   - Three concise bilingual reviews
   - Focus on sparkle, craftsmanship, communication, and custom fit

8. Domain section
   - Showcase `xingyuejewelry.com`
   - Position it as the official online boutique

9. Footer
   - Brand summary
   - Product links
   - Service links
   - Contact/social placeholders

## Technical Design

- Framework: Next.js
- Styling: Tailwind CSS
- App type: static marketing homepage
- Page route: `/`
- Components may stay inline if the project is small, but repeated sections should use arrays and mapped UI for maintainability.
- Use semantic sections, accessible button/link labels, and responsive layouts for mobile and desktop.

## Content Requirements

- Avoid generic luxury filler where possible.
- Mention the real product focus: moissanite, lab-grown diamond, S925 silver settings, and custom K gold settings.
- Keep bilingual copy compact so the page still feels premium.
- Use placeholder certificate wording carefully and avoid claiming universal GIA/IGI certification for every item.
